About us

Everyday objects, made to be used

Meridian began as a single shelf in a shared studio: notebooks we actually wrote in, a lamp that did not glare, a bottle that did not leak. We now curate that same short list for anyone who would rather buy one good version of a thing than three forgettable ones.
